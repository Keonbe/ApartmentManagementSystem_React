# Manager Interview (Round 2) vs. Current System Policies

The apartment manager's latest interview (30 questions, originally in Tagalog) is translated below and compared with what the system does today.

Sources checked: `SetupDB.sql` (settings, rooms, house rules), `backend/config.php` (move-in billing, refunds), `backend/contract_template.php`, `backend/api/rent_application.php`, `submit_split_payment.php`, `create_reservation.php`, and `panelist_feature_checklist.md`. The first comparison was made from the code; the rows marked *Resolved* were changed afterwards (Phases 1-5 of the manager-interview alignment) and checked against the dev server and the API test suite. Rows the code could not confirm say so.

**Match key:** ✅ matches · ⚠️ partial or different · ❌ conflict · ➖ no system policy

## Summary

Status after Phases 1-5, the Q13/Q22 follow-up and the Q26 backup runbook: **30 of 30 answers match**; Q26 is matched on paper, and the operator still has to switch the backup schedule on.

- **✅ 30 match.** 17 already matched (rent amounts, the ₱8,000 move-in, non-refundable deposit, no late fees, proof-based payments, one record replacing the sheets). 10 were changed to match (Q1 including the previous address, Q5, Q6, Q9, Q12, Q13, Q17, Q22, Q24, Q30). 1 was documented (Q26, production backup and restore). 3 stay as they are by decision (Q2, Q3, Q4).
- **Resolved conflicts (were ❌):**
  - NBI clearance required (Q1, Q2, Q5): the documents are admin-configurable and default to the valid ID only.
  - Children under 12 banned on every floor (Q6): now no children under 5 on 3F; floors and age are editable in Settings -> Minors & Pets, no building-wide minimum.
  - Visitor registration required (Q24): the existing Services toggle turns it off, and the visitor house rule is hidden while it is off.
- **Resolved partials (were ⚠️):** previous address (now required on the application), installments (part-payment ledger, the move-in invoice included), utility billing (provider-bill entry is the default), deposit deductions (itemized with a reason per line).
- **Decided, kept as is:** the admin review step before a unit is offered (Q2, Q3) and no separate owner-approval role (Q4).
- **Documented (was ⚠️):** production backup and restore (Q26), in [backup_and_restore.md](../deployment/backup_and_restore.md). Nothing is scheduled yet: the operator enables the nightly job and runs the first restore drill.
- **Added:** invoice adjustment history with a required reason (Q13) and a reused-payment-reference setting (Q22).
